# Field Staff Quick Start Guide — Childcare Support App

Welcome! This app helps you record child assessments quickly on your phone or tablet—even in areas with weak or no internet.

---

## 1. Before You Start (2 Minutes)
1. **Open the app link** shared by your trainer on Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
2. **Sign in** using your assigned state credentials (keep your State & SR details selected if prompted once).
3. **Turn on Location (GPS)** on your phone so **Fetch Live Address** can fill address details faster.
4. **Best tip for offline clinics/visits:** Open the app once while you have internet before leaving for the field.

---

## 2. Understanding the Home Screen Circles
- **FORMS TO FINISH (Green):** Incomplete forms saved on your phone. Tap **Continue forms** to finish them.
- **WAITING TO SEND (Amber):** Completed forms saved safely on your phone that have not reached the server yet (for example, when offline). The app retries automatically when internet returns. **Do NOT clear browser data** while forms are here.
- **PENDINGS (Rose):** Submitted forms needing **Missing documents** or **Bank details needed**. Tap either row to add them later.

---

## 3. Filling a Child Assessment Form
1. **Child & Caregiver Details:**
   - Enter the exact **ART / Reference ID** (at least 3 characters), **Child Full Name**, and **Date of Birth**.
   - Enter the caregiver's **10-digit mobile number**. If they do not have one, tick **"No phone available"** and add an alternate contact if known.
2. **Address (`Fetch Live Address`):**
   - Stand near the home/centre with GPS on and tap **Fetch Live Address** (wait up to 30 seconds for a fix).
   - Or type the **6-digit Pin Code** manually—District and State will auto-fill when matched.
   - Check **Taluk / Mandal / Block** carefully (look for the **"Please verify"** tag) and edit it if needed.
3. **Document Verification Proofs (Mandatory):**
   - You **must** provide at least ONE of the following before submitting:
     - Upload a clear photo/PDF of the **School Fee Receipt**, **OR**
     - Upload the **Previous Year Marksheet**, **OR**
     - Write clear **Remarks** (minimum 3 characters, e.g., `"Receipt pending from school office"`).
   - **File limit:** Up to **2.5 MB** (`JPEG`, `PNG`, or `PDF`). Phone photos are compressed automatically before upload.
4. **Caregiver Consent & Final Review:**
   - Confirm caregiver consent, capture the signature, review all sections, and tap **Submit Assessment**.

---

## 4. What If Internet Fails?
- If signal drops during submission, **nothing is lost**. Your form stays in **Waiting to send** and retries automatically with backoff.
- When you reach Wi-Fi or 4G, open the app and check that **Waiting to send** drops to **0** (or tap **View outbox** -> **Sync Now**).

## 5. Need Help?
- Do not delete browser history/site data while drafts or outbox items exist.
- Contact your State Coordinator or Trainer if an item turns red (**Failed / Conflict**) in **View outbox**.
